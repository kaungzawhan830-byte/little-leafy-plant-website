Indoor Plants Guide(Over View)

Snake Plant

Light: Low to bright indirect

Water: Every 2-3 weeks

Care: Great for beginners, purifies air

Monstera Deliciosa

Light: Bright indirect

Water: Weekly in summer

Care: Loves humidity, wipe leaves

ZZ Plant

Light: Low to medium

Water: Every 3-4 weeks

Care: Drought tolerant, slow grower

Pothos

Light: Low to bright indirect

Water: When soil is dry

Care: Trailing vine, easy to propagate

Outdoor Plants Guide(Over View)

Lavender

Light: Full sun

Water: Moderate, drought tolerant

Care: Well-draining soil, prune annually

Hydrangea

Light: Morning sun, afternoon shade

Water: Keep soil moist

Care: Acidic soil for blue flowers

Boxwood

Light: Full sun to part shade

Water: Regular watering

Care: Great for hedges and topiary

Japanese Maple

Light: Dappled shade

Water: Keep evenly moist

Care: Protect from strong winds

Succulents & Cacti Guide(Over View)

Echeveria

Light: Bright light

Water: When soil is completely dry

Care: Avoid water on leaves

Aloe Vera

Light: Bright indirect to direct

Water: Every 3 weeks

Care: Medicinal properties

Barrel Cactus

Light: Full sun

Water: Monthly in summer

Care: Winter dormant - no water

String of Pearls

Light: Bright indirect

Water: Every 2-3 weeks

Care: Trailing succulent

About Little Leafy

Bringing nature's beauty into your home and garden since 2018

Our Story

Little Leafy began in 2018 with a simple mission: to make quality plants accessible to everyone while promoting the joy and benefits of living with plants.

From Humble Beginnings

What started as a small neighborhood plant stand has grown into Yangon's beloved plant nursery. Our founder has always believed that plants make people happier and spaces more beautiful.

With this vision, Little Leafy started with just 50 plants in a backyard. Today, we offer hundreds of plant varieties and have helped thousands of customers create their own green sanctuaries.

Our Mission & Vision

At Little Leafy, we're committed to providing the highest quality plants along with the knowledge needed to help them thrive. We believe that connecting with nature improves our wellbeing.

Our vision is to create a greener, more beautiful world—one plant at a time. We aim to inspire and enable everyone to experience the joys of plant parenthood.

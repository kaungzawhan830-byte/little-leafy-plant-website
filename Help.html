Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number and link to track your package. You can also log in to your account to view the status of all your orders.

What's your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for most items. Plants must be in healthy condition with original packaging. Please contact our customer service to initiate a return and we'll provide you with instructions.

How often should I water my new plant?

Watering needs vary by plant type. As a general rule, check the soil moisture by sticking your finger about an inch deep. If it feels dry, it's time to water. Most houseplants prefer thorough watering followed by allowing the soil to dry out slightly between waterings.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we only ship within the United States due to agricultural restrictions on live plants. We're working to expand our shipping options in the future.

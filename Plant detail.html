About Monstera 'Split-Leaf'

The Monstera 'Split-Leaf', also known as Monstera deliciosa, is a tropical plant native to Central America. It's famous for its large, glossy leaves with natural splits and holes, giving it the nickname "Swiss Cheese Plant".

This easy-care plant thrives in bright, indirect light and prefers slightly moist soil. It's an excellent air-purifying plant that can grow quite large, making it a stunning statement piece in any room.

Our Monstera plants are carefully selected for their health and beauty, ensuring you receive a plant that will thrive in your home environment.

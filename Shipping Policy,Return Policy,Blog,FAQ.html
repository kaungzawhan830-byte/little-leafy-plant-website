Frequently Asked Questions

Plant Care

How often should I water my new plant?

Watering needs vary by plant type. We include specific care instructions with each order. As a general rule, check the soil moisture weekly by sticking your finger about an inch into the soil - if it's dry, it's time to water. Most indoor plants prefer thorough watering followed by allowing the soil to dry out slightly between waterings.

What if my plant starts looking unhealthy?

Contact us with photos and we'll help diagnose the issue. Many common problems can be fixed with simple adjustments to light or watering. Yellow leaves often indicate overwatering, while brown crispy tips may mean the plant needs more humidity. We offer free plant consultations for all our customers.
